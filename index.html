Who we are

Our Story

Made with love, served with a smile

The Bubble Hub Melkbos is a cozy café created for people who love unique drinks, desserts and relaxing moments. From refreshing bubble teas to handmade waffles, creamy milkshakes and tasty snacks, every item is made to bring happiness.

Our friendly team prepares everything fresh in-house, daily — because good vibes start with good food.

  • 🧇 Hand-made waffles & desserts
  • 💗 A cozy spot for friends & family
